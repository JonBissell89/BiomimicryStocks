# Round 4 — Group 10 notes (diagnostics + SSII)

## NTRA — Natera (~$332, ~$47B) — ADVANCE, 89
Sells Panorama NIPT, Horizon carrier screening, Signatera tumor-informed MRD, Prospera transplant rejection. Q2 2026: revenue ~$753M (+38% YoY), >1.04M tests processed, gross margin ~65%, positive cash flow; FY26 guidance raised $100M to $2.85–2.91B. Signatera ASP ~$1,275 heading to ~$2,000; clinical MRD units +56% YoY. Regulatory momentum: FDA CDx approval in bladder cancer, Japan CRC approval, EU IVDR, NCCN Category 1. Cost compression is real: MRD-negative patients (82%) get no benefit from adjuvant chemo — Signatera lets them skip it (2-yr DFS 89.7% vs 89.5%), avoiding chemo cost and toxicity; earlier recurrence detection improves outcomes. Moat: bespoke tumor-informed assay, deepest evidence base (GALAXY/CIRCULATE), payer coverage, scale vs Guardant Reveal/Haystack MD.

## VCYT — Veracyte (~$41.25, ~$2.6B) — ADVANCE, 82
Sells Afirma (thyroid nodule classifier), Decipher Prostate (treatment-intensity genomic classifier), plus newly launched Prosigna (breast) and TrueMRD (bladder recurrence monitoring). Q2 2026: revenue $150.3M (+15%), testing revenue +19%, GAAP profitable (adj EPS $0.54), FY26 guidance raised to $590–596M with adjusted EBITDA >26%. Decipher +20% revenue (~30K tests), Afirma +18% (~18.6K tests). Cost compression: Afirma is cost-effective at ~$4,234 per unnecessary thyroid surgery avoided; Decipher steers low-risk prostate patients to active surveillance instead of surgery/radiation. Moat: deepest evidence base and NCCN positioning for its classifiers; competitors ThyroSeq and Oncotype GPS exist but Veracyte holds share leadership. Profitable, cash-rich, no meaningful debt. Growth mid-teens, not hyper-scale.

## HTFL — Heartflow (~$48.94, ~$4.3B) — ADVANCE, 82
Cloud AI analysis of standard coronary CT scans: FFRCT (physiologic significance of stenosis) plus fast-growing Plaque quantification. Q2 2026: revenue $64.1M (+48%, fastest in 8 quarters), US +51%, gross margin 83.3% (+770bps), Plaque revenue $7.8M (~$4M above plan). FY26 guidance raised to $246–250M (+40–42%), ~82% GM; cash-flow profitability targeted mid-2028 (net loss still ~$118M TTM; IPO Aug 2025 funded balance sheet). System economics: FFRCT-guided pathways cancel a large share of planned invasive angiograms (PLATFORM trial), shifting diagnosis from cath lab to software at near-zero marginal cost — genuine cost compression with capex-light global scaling. Moat: FDA clearances, chest-pain guideline inclusion, established reimbursement, 10+ yrs clinical evidence; Cleerly/Elucid are credible plaque-AI rivals.

## BLLN — BillionToOne (~$97.25, ~$4.6B) — ADVANCE, 80
Proprietary QCT single-molecule counting powers UNITY (the only NIPT that screens single-gene diseases — SMA, CF, sickle cell — plus fetal antigen/aneuploidy from one maternal draw) and Northstar oncology liquid biopsy. Q2 2026: revenue $109.4M (+64%; volume +35%, ASP +21% to $551), 196K tests, gross margin 70% (+5pts), operating income $5.5M — turned profitable. Prenatal revenue $94.2M (+55%); Northstar Select QC failure <1% vs 15–30% for tissue assays. FY26 guidance reiterated $450–465M (stock sold off post-earnings from a $150 peak). Cost compression: one blood draw replaces the mother-plus-partner carrier-screening cascade and reduces invasive diagnostics; oncology monitoring at lower failure/turnaround cost. Moat: QCT patents and category-of-one product; giants (Natera, Labcorp) loom but haven't matched single-gene NIPT.

## CDNA — CareDx (~$47.50, ~$2.5B) — CUT, 75
AlloSure/AlloMap donor-derived cfDNA surveillance for kidney/heart/lung transplants, plus HLA lab products and digital solutions. Q2 2026: revenue $132M (+52%), testing services $100M (+61%), non-GAAP GM 74%, adjusted EBITDA $25M (19%); FY26 guidance raised to $490–500M. Stock +266% in a year. System case is real: dd-cfDNA flags rejection before kidney-function decline (4–6x graft-loss risk when elevated), reduces surveillance biopsies, and preserving a graft avoids ~$90K/yr dialysis. Decisive weakness: thin moat — Natera's Prospera competes head-to-head with stronger tech momentum; CareDx lost its false-advertising award (Supreme Court cert denied June 2026) and has a history of billing/regulatory headwinds. Single-niche TAM with a superior rival already advanced in this group.

## CAI — Caris Life Sciences (~$26.82, ~$7.6B) — ADVANCE, 81
AI TechBio running whole-exome + whole-transcriptome sequencing (WES/WTS) on every solid-tumor case, matching patients to the right therapy first time. Q2 2026: revenue $263.7M (+45%), molecular profiling $252.3M (+55%), ~59,200 clinical cases (+18%), record blended ASP >$3,850, GAAP GM 68%, fifth straight quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA ($55.7M) and positive FCF ($6.4M). FY26 guidance raised to $1.03–1.04B, excluding new launches (Caris Detect, ChromoSeq, MI Clarity V2). TTM revenue ~$990M (+85%). Cost compression: one comprehensive test replaces sequential single-biomarker panels and averts months of ineffective five-figure-per-month therapy. Moat: largest WES/WTS clinico-genomic database plus AI models and pharma partnerships; Tempus and Foundation Medicine are real competition. Heavy pre-IPO losses now behind it.

## SSII — SS Innovations (~$3.58, ~$734M) — CUT, 63
SSi Mantra surgical robot + SSi Mudra instruments, priced roughly a third of a da Vinci to democratize robotic surgery in emerging markets. Commercialization is real, not vapor: Q2 2026 revenue $13.9M (+39%), H1 $25.0M (+66%), 30 installs in Q2, installed base 224 systems across 12 countries, 12,272 cumulative surgeries incl. 175 telesurgeries. Cost-compression thesis (cheap capable robot, cardiac capability) is genuine. But the financials are fragile: Q2 net loss $2.7M (widening), gross margin contracted to 50.9%, cash $13.6M against a $14.6M bank-overdraft liability (net negative cash), ~6M new shares issued in six months. FDA 510(k) review slipped to Q1 2027; CE mark targeted end-2026. Thin moat vs Intuitive, Medtronic Hugo, CMR and cheap Chinese rivals (MicroPort Toumai). Subscale hardware model with funding risk.
