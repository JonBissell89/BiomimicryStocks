## TMDX — TransMedics Group — 80 — ADVANCE
Price ~$90.56, mkt cap ~$3.14B. Q2 2026 revenue $189.9M (+21% YoY, +9% QoQ), EPS $0.41, GM 59.6%; FY26 guidance raised to $737–757M (+22–25%). ## CBLL — CeriBell — 80 — ADVANCE
Price ~$24.21, mkt cap ~$929M. Q2 2026 revenue $28.1M (+33% YoY; product +33%, subscription +30%), 712 active accounts, gross margin 92%; net loss $19.3M as opex grew 37%.
