## CP — Canadian Pacific Kansas City (NYSE)
Price $94.71; market cap $83.19B (Aug 26, 2026). Q2 2026 (Jul 30): revenue CAD 4.2B (+13% YoY), record; RTMs +4%; core adjusted diluted EPS $1.27; core adjusted OR 61.6% (up 90bp — cost discipline slipped slightly). Broke all-time Canadian grain record: 30.66M tonnes in 2025-26 crop year. Moat: the ONLY single-line railway connecting Canada, US and Mexico — irreplicable right-of-way plus near-impossible regulatory path for any new entrant; single-line service removes interchange cost/delay vs truck and rival rail pairings. System balance: rail ~3-4x more fuel-efficient than trucking. Key risk: proposed UP-NS transcontinental merger (STB schedule restarted Aug 18, 2026) could structurally disadvantage CPKC's network; CPKC opposing. Growth guided to accelerate H2 2026.

## CNI — Canadian National Railway (NYSE)
Price $127.54; market cap $76.71B (Aug 26, 2026). Q2 2026 (Jul 22-24): revenue C$4.75B (+11.2% YoY, beat by 5.4%); adjusted EPS US$1.50 (beat); adjusted OR 62.2% (+50bp). ## NSC — Norfolk Southern (NYSE)
Price $352.68; market cap $79.21B (Aug 26, 2026). Q2 2026 (Jul 23): record revenue $3.5B; adjusted operating income $1.2B; adjusted OR 65.5% — worst of the Class I majors covered here and 210bp WORSE YoY on fuel/inflation; adjusted diluted EPS $3.52. GAAP results still carry merger-related expenses, restructuring, and Eastern Ohio (East Palestine) incident costs. The stock is now effectively a merger-arb instrument: Union Pacific's proposed acquisition is before the STB (proceeding resumed Aug 18, 2026; comments into early 2027), so the equity's fate is binary — absorbed into UNP if approved, or left as the structurally weakest major if rejected. That plus the group-lagging efficiency makes it unfit for a compounder list despite the essential eastern network.

## UNP — Union Pacific (NYSE)
Price $310.62; market cap $184.53B (Aug 26, 2026) — largest listed railroad. Q2 2026 (Jul 23): operating revenue record $6.9B (+12%); adjusted diluted EPS $3.41 (+13%); adjusted OR 59.2% — best in class, improved despite a 120bp fuel headwind; net income $2.0B; domestic intermodal set its fourth straight record quarter; FY2026 EPS growth outlook raised to high single digits. Moat: western-duopoly franchise (23 states, Mexico gateways, Powder River, LA/Long Beach access) that cannot be replicated. Pending acquisition of Norfolk Southern would create the first true US transcontinental single-line railroad — removing interchange friction (system cost compression) but carrying heavy STB/regulatory and integration risk into 2027; an interoperability pact with CN eases the path. Strongest financials in the group.

## WAB — Wabtec (NYSE)
Price $300.54; market cap $50.76B (Aug 26, 2026). Q2 2026 (Jul 29): sales +17.5% to $3.18B; adjusted EPS +21.6% to $2.76; adjusted gross margin 36.7% (+190bp); adjusted operating margin 21.9% (+80bp). Total backlog a record $30.93B, +41.7% YoY (freight $25.3B, transit $5.6B). Guidance raised again: FY2026 revenue ~$12.5B (+11.5%), adjusted EPS $10.60-10.90. Moat: NA locomotive duopoly (vs Progress Rail/CAT), ~23k-unit installed base feeding decades of high-margin parts/services/modernizations, plus digital (Trip Optimizer, PTC) and FLXdrive battery-electric — kit that cuts customer fuel burn (real cost compression). Unlike the railroads it scales as a global product/services platform (India, Brazil, Kazakhstan orders) rather than via localized capex. Risks: 40x trailing PE; cyclical NA locomotive deliveries; customer consolidation (UP-NS) concentrating buyer power.
