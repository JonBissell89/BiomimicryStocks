## LDGYY — Landis+Gyr Group AG
Sells smart electricity meters, AMI networks, meter-data-management software and grid-edge intelligence; now focused on Americas/APAC after divesting the entire EMEA metering business to AURELIUS (EV $215M, closed 31 Mar 2026) and planning a US listing. FY2025 (ended Mar 2026) revenue $1,166M (+4.2%), adj. EBITDA $167.5M (+10.9%); backlog $3.9B (+26% YoY, 40% software) — a real installed-base/certification moat with multi-year utility contracts. But momentum is fading: Q1 FY2026 revenue -6.8% to $232M, order intake -2.7%, book-to-bill 0.95x, and FY2026 guidance implies revenue decline ($1.075–1.125B). Record 37.4% gross margin shows pricing power, yet a shrinking top line disqualifies it as a compounder. Price ~$12.75 ADR; mkt cap ~$1.5B.

## WSIOF — Wasion Holdings (3393.HK)
China's leading AMI/smart-meter maker (electricity, water, gas, power distribution) with fast-growing overseas business across Africa, Latin America, Europe, Asia. FY2025 revenue RMB 10.07B (+15.6%); net profit guided RMB 1.00-1.06B, +42-50% YoY; pays a dividend (~3.7% yield). Order momentum is the group's best: ranked #1 by value (RMB 383M) in State Grid's 2026 centralized smart-meter tender, and won Mexico CFE's FY2026 tender worth over RMB 695M (~$96M). Moat is scale/cost leadership plus State Grid and international utility qualifications rather than deep IP; meters are semi-commoditized, but Wasion keeps winning share. Price HK$17.29 (26 Aug 2026), mkt cap HK$18.1B (~US$2.3B); WSIOF ~US$2.21.

## DLEGF — Delta Electronics (Thailand) PCL
Makes power electronics (AI server/data-center power, EV mobility, ICT infrastructure, automation); the Delta group's China+1 manufacturing hub. Q2 2026 revenue $2,011M, +50.7% YoY on AI/data-center demand (data center +79% QoQ); 2025 revenue THB 198.2B (+20.3%), net profit +42%. But Q2 net profit fell 35% QoQ on raw-material shortages, supply constraints and inventory provisions. Moat: scale leadership in AI server PSUs (Delta/LiteOn/FSP ~35% share, Delta #1), position inside NVIDIA's 800VDC power ecosystem, moving into grey-space vs Vertiv with solid-state transformers/fuel cells. Momentum: $550M capex for new factories; double-digit 2026 growth guided. Risks: component-level commoditization, parent-subsidiary structure, extreme valuation. Price $8.00, mkt cap $94.2B (25 Aug 2026).

## ABHBY — Alfen N.V.
Dutch maker of compact smart-grid substations, grid-connected battery storage (TheBattery) and EV chargers. H1 2026 revenue €261.5M (+24%): Energy Storage +87.8%, Smart Grid +14.9%, but EV Charging -17.4% (€51.1M) amid home-charging commoditization and a portfolio refresh. Adjusted EBITDA only €16.4M (6.3% margin); swung to net cash €6.2M from €20.7M net debt via inventory reduction; backlog ~€150M; 2026 guidance reaffirmed but shares fell 6.3% on soft outlook. Moat is real but narrow: certified framework supplier to Dutch DSOs (Liander/Enexis/Stedin) with a large installed substation base — yet geographically Netherlands-centric, and storage/EV segments face heavy substitution. A turnaround, not a compounder. Price $6.94 ADR, mkt cap ~$303M (Aug 2026).

## HRASF — Hera S.p.A. (HER.MI)
Bologna-based leading Italian multi-utility: gas/electricity networks and supply, integrated water cycle, waste/environment (circular-economy leader). H1 2026: EBITDA €727M (+0.7%; adjusted +7.5% to €715.5M), adjusted net profit +15.8% to €227.6M; gross investments +23.3% to €510.5M; net debt €4.25B (2.75x EBITDA). Business plan allocates ~€5.5B of capex (≈€2.2B to networks/smart grids). Moat is genuine — regulated concessions, an entrenched installed network/water/waste base, municipal shareholder anchor — but this is a regulated compounder of the 3-5%/yr kind: Networks EBITDA actually declined in H1, growth relies on tariff (RAB) accretion and waste M&A. Fails the scale-velocity test for a Top-25 compounder list. Price €3.91 (HER.MI, late Jul 2026), mkt cap €5.79B (~$6.7B); HRASF ADR-equivalent ~$4.55.

## LGRDY — Legrand SA
Global #1 in low-voltage electrical infrastructure (wiring devices, cable management, busway, UPS/PDU white-space for data centers). H1 2026: sales €5,400M, +13.1% reported / +17.4% ex-FX (9.8% organic + 6.9% M&A); adjusted operating margin 20.8%; net profit +11.2% to €698M; FCF €488M. Data centers now 32% of revenue growing >30% organically; FY2026 guidance raised to 16-19% ex-FX growth (from 10-15%); seven acquisitions adding ~€450M sales. Moat is exactly the installed-base/standards kind: country-specific electrical codes, electrician brand habit, distributor lock-in, plus fast-turn DC busway/PDU franchise. Risks: construction-market cyclicality outside DC, M&A integration. Decades of profitable compounding, minimal dilution. Price $31.32 ADR (19 Aug 2026), mkt cap ~$42B.

## SHLS — Shoals Technologies Group
US leader in electrical balance-of-system (EBOS) for utility-scale solar and battery storage: patented plug-and-play Big Lead Assembly harnesses that cut field labor (fewer electricians, above-ground wiring) — genuine cost-per-watt compression. Q2 2026: revenue $163.4M (+47% YoY), adjusted EBITDA $31.6M (+28%), adjusted EPS $0.12; record backlog/awarded orders $801M, book-to-bill 1.3x, ~$700M shipping by Q2 2027. FY2026 reaffirmed: $600-640M revenue (+30%), adj EBITDA $118-132M. Moat: patents (actively litigated), UL-certified designs, EPC installed-base familiarity; but competitors (Voltage et al.) encroach and litigation costs pinch margins. Key risk: dependence on US utility-scale solar policy cycle. Price ~$6.80 (24 Aug 2026), mkt cap ~$1.2B.

## GNSPF — GenusPlus Group (ASX:GNP)
Australian power-infrastructure contractor: transmission lines, substations, distribution, BESS EPC, comms. FY26 (Jun-2026): revenue A$1.281B (+70.5%), normalised EBITDA A$100.8M (+49.6%, ~7.9% margin), underlying NPAT A$54.7M (+44%); order book A$2.2B ex-recurring; cash A$476M after a A$195.6M equity raise (note dilution); dividend +55.6%. FY27 guidance: EBITDA A$200-205M (~2x) with ~A$764M recurring revenue. Wins: A$110M Koolunga 800MWh BESS, A$55M Rio Tinto Millstream 220kV substation under a Master Construction Agreement, Rewiring-the-Nation transmission work. Best order momentum in the group, but the moat is thin: accreditation and workforce, not IP/standards — competitive, labor-heavy, Australia-localized project contracting with thin margins. Price GNSPF $6.91 (~A$11 on ASX), mkt cap ~US$1.3B (A$1.99B).
