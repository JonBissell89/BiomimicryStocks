## MBRFF — Mo-BRUK S.A. (WSE: MBR) — 78 — ADVANCE
Poland's leading industrial/hazardous waste processor: incineration, solidification/stabilization (waste-to-artificial-aggregate), RDF. Q1 2026 revenue PLN 79.0M (+32.6% y/y), net profit PLN 15.6M (+23.9%), EBITDA +25.1%; tons processed 82.9k vs 73.2k. FY2025 revenue PLN ~320M (+12.7%). Regulatory permit moat: scarce incineration/solidification permits; dominant bidder in Poland's state-funded "ecological bomb" illegal-dump cleanup super-cycle (PLN 300M/yr ring-fenced through 2027; ~PLN 127M of 2026 remediation revenue expected). Genuinely closes material loops (waste to aggregate/energy) and removes toxic legacy sites. Risks: Q3 2025 one-off PLN 65.2M environmental-fee write-down; expansion is capex/permit-bound and mostly domestic — scale velocity limited. Price ~370 PLN (~$101 ADR-equiv), mcap ~PLN 1.30B (~$356M).

## TMRAY — Tomra Systems ASA (OSE: TOM) — 84 — ADVANCE
World leader (~80% share) in reverse vending machines plus sensor-based recycling and food sorting. Q2 2026 revenue EUR 405M vs 325M (+25% y/y); Collection segment +45% to EUR 246M, driven by new deposit-return systems in Poland (7,000+ RVMs, 800M containers collected since launch), Portugal, Singapore, Romania; ~1,200-RVM order from a major UK retailer ahead of UK's 2027 DRS. Food +5%; Recycling -11% (weak Asia). This is the archetypal exportable circular-economy platform: machines + service ship into each new DRS jurisdiction without building a utility. Moat: installed base, uptime/service network, regulator trust. Risks: Recycling cyclicality, DRS timing dependence. Price ~103 NOK (TMRAY ADR ~$10); mcap ~NOK 32.3B (~$3.2B).

## TSPCF — Cleanaway Waste Management (ASX: CWY) — 69 — CUT
Australia's largest waste company: solid waste collection, landfills, liquid/industrial services, growing recycling and resource-recovery infrastructure (Blueprint 2030). FY26 (Aug 2026): gross revenue +13.5% to A$4.37B, underlying EBIT +14.2% to A$470.2M, FCF +63.7% to A$213.8M; but statutory NPAT -37.2% to A$98.5M on A$124.6M of one-offs; FY27 EBIT guided A$500-530M. Moat is real: scarce landfill licenses, network density, regional oligopoly (competitors Veolia/Suez, Remondis, Visy). Yet it hardens only Australia's network — capex-heavy, geography-bound, no product/licensing export path; circularity is partial (landfill still core profit engine). Stock -19% over 12 months; price A$2.34, mcap A$5.24B (~US$3.4B).

## BIRMF — Biorem Inc (TSXV: BRM) — 73 — ADVANCE
Canadian clean-tech engineer of biological air-pollution control: biofilters, biotrickling filters, multi-stage systems and biogas desulfurization that strip odors, VOCs and hazardous air pollutants — the workhorse installed at wastewater treatment plants and industrial/biogas sites. Biology replaces chemical scrubbing: lower energy, no chemical consumables — real lifecycle cost compression — plus recurring media-replacement/service revenue on the installed base. Q2 2026 (Aug): revenue C$15.7M (+66% y/y), net earnings ~$2.7M (EPS $0.12), gross margin 31%, 20 new projects, backlog near-record C$75.5M (Q1 record C$77M; Q1 revenue +44%). Profitable, decades of references, regulation-driven demand. Risks: project lumpiness, microcap liquidity, mostly North American sales. Price ~C$3.85 (near 52-wk high; BIRMF ~US$2.80), mcap ~C$60M.
