## PIFFY — PT Indofood CBP Sukses Makmur (ADR)
Indonesia's dominant packaged-food maker: instant noodles (Indomie), dairy, snacks, seasonings, nutrition, beverages. Q1 2026 net sales +8% YoY to IDR 21.72T; net profit -3% to IDR 2.57T (12% margin vs 13%) on higher expenses; growth broad-based except beverages (-3%). FY2025 gross margin ~35.2%, EBITDA margin ~22.7%; debt/equity ~66% after the $3B Pinehill acquisition (Indomie in Saudi/Egypt/Kenya/Turkey/Serbia/Morocco; 12 plants, 550M-consumer addressable market) — a related-party purchase from the Salim group, a persistent governance overhang. Strong brand/distribution moat and permanent need for cheap calories, but product is sodium-heavy processed food; system-improvement case is weak. ADR ~$15.38; market cap ~$5.0B.

## FLO — Flowers Foods
US #2 packaged bakery (Nature's Own, Dave's Killer Bread, Wonder, Canyon Bakehouse, Simple Mills). Q2 2026 (reported 8/20/26): net sales $1.193B, -4.0% YoY on -5.8% volume; adj EPS $0.21 (miss); FY26 guidance cut to $5.07-5.14B sales and $0.75-0.85 adj EPS. FY2025 net income fell 66% to $84M. Bread category in structural volume decline (budget-pressured shoppers, private label, low-carb/GLP-1 shift). Long-term debt ~$1.75B after the $795M Simple Mills acquisition (Feb 2025) vs $1.52B market cap; dividend effectively reduced (annual $0.50, ~7% yield). Brands and DSD network give some moat, but volumes, margins and leverage all deteriorating. Price $7.17; market cap $1.52B (8/26/26).

## LEGH — Legacy Housing
Vertically integrated Texas/Georgia manufactured-home builder (4th-largest US producer) with owned/independent dealers, captive chattel and MH-community lending, and land development. Q2 2026 (8/13/26): record — net revenue $66.3M +32.3% YoY, product sales +40% (718 homes shipped incl. 113 units of a 380-unit workforce-housing contract running through 2026), net income $23.5M +59.9%, EPS $0.99 vs $0.64 est; revenue per unit ~$74.9k. FY2025 was soft (revenue $164.6M, -10.7%). Book value ~$22.20/share, minimal debt, no dilution. Factory-built housing halves cost per square foot vs site-built — direct affordability compression in a chronic US housing shortage. Risks: Clayton/Cavco/Champion scale, credit risk in the loan book, cyclicality. Price $28.72; market cap ~$625M.

OTC price $3.19; market cap ~$2.2B (8/19/26).

H1 2026: sales €23.6B, +0.7% organic (Q2 accelerated to +3.5% as construction rebounded); EBITDA €3.6B, 15.4% margin (-60bps); recurring net income €1.7B; FCF €2.1B; FY26 guidance >15% margin reaffirmed. ## GRPRF — Grupo Rotoplas (BMV: AGUA)
Latin America's leading water-solutions maker: storage tanks/cisterns (the default "tinaco" brand in Mexico), purification, heaters, pipes; 18 plants, 14 countries; services platform incl. bebbia purified-water subscription (~72,000 families) and RSA treatment plants. System-balance case is genuinely strong: decentralized storage buffers intermittent municipal supply, and bebbia undercuts bottled/garrafon water, cutting plastic and trucking — water-security adaptation for underserved households, not scarcity profiteering. But execution lags the mission: Q2'26 net sales MXN 3.0B +3.4%, EBITDA margin 13.4% (improving); H1 sales +2.2%; Q2 net LOSS MXN 201M on Argentina monetary-position costs; FY2025 revenue MXN 10.69B (-4.5%) with net income down 75% to ~MXN 13M. Years of flat revenue and near-zero earnings. OTC last $0.82 (thin; AGUA MXN 12.18); market cap ~US$370M.

## SBDHF — SHO-BOND Holdings (TSE: 1414)
Japan's leading specialist in repair and seismic reinforcement of existing civil infrastructure (bridges, tunnels, expressways) — integrated proprietary repair materials plus engineering, 60+ years in the niche. TTM revenue ¥89.2B (-1.7%), net profit ¥15.44B (+2.5%), net margin ~17.3% — extraordinary for construction and evidence of pricing power; FY6/26 forecast ¥91.0B sales, FY6/27 guide ¥90.0B sales / ¥15.5B profit (flat). System-balance case is first-rate: extending asset life at a fraction of replacement cost is circularity and resilience (Japan's 50-year-old bridge stock keeps growing; national resilience budgets fund the work). Watch items: recent order backlog declined; domestic concentration; labor-constrained, slow topline. Local price ~¥1,246 (5/22/26); market cap ~¥251B (~US$1.7B). SBDHF OTC last trade $8.90 (3/26/26, thin); USD market cap ~$1.6B.
