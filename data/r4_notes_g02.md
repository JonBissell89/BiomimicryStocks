## MBLY (Mobileye) — price $8.54, mcap ~$7.26B (Aug 26, 2026)
Sells EyeQ ADAS chips/software (~$46 ASP) moving up to Surround ADAS (~$125), SuperVision, and Chauffeur (~$2,500-3,000). Q2 2026 revenue $508M (~flat YoY); FY26 guide ~$1.995B (+4-7%); adjusted operating profit +46%; $210M H1 operating cash flow; large cash pile, no debt. Real cost compression: camera-first ADAS cuts crash/insurance costs system-wide and is regulation-driven (EU GSR/NCAP); robotaxi cost/mile unproven at scale. Expansion: EyeQ6H win with top-10 US automaker (~19M future units), three Surround ADAS wins (2028), Porsche SuperVision (2027), VW MOIA robotaxi in 5-6 cities by end-2027. Risks: stagnant growth, Chinese OEM in-housing (Horizon), Nvidia/Qualcomm, Intel ~88% ownership overhang, founder-CEO stepping down.

## WTCHF (WiseTech Global) — OTC $31.08 (Aug 25 close), mcap ~$9.75B; ASX fell ~10% to AUD 40.89 on FY26 results (Aug 26, 2026)
CargoWise is the operating system for global freight forwarders (DSV, K+N, top-25 forwarders standardized on it). FY26 (ended Jun 30): revenue AUD 1.396B +79% (first full e2open year); CargoWise organic +11% — decelerating; underlying EBITDA AUD 644.5M +56%; operating cash flow AUD 564M. Real cost compression: Container Transport Optimization targets empty container movements/congestion in landside logistics; e2open/INTTRA (~18.5% of ocean bookings) adds network data; forwarder productivity gains are proven. Moat: deep customs/compliance content, extreme switching costs. Red flags: ACCC executed a search warrant Aug 19, 2026 (competition probe); founder Richard White exited exec chair July; ~2,000 layoffs; profit missed; e2open debt/amortization drag.

## BSY (Bentley Systems) — price $36.94, mcap $11.46B (Aug 26, 2026)
Infrastructure engineering software (MicroStation, ProjectWise, SYNCHRO, AssetWise, iTwin/Cesium digital twins) for roads, rail, water, grid. Q2 2026: revenue $410.7M +12.8%; ARR $1.536B, growth accelerating to 12%; NRR 109%; TTM net income $290M; pays dividend. Cost compression is quantified and physical: Baosteel cut rework 15% to 0.8% and welding costs -35% (RMB 49M saved); ACCIONA M80 Ring Road AU$105.8M projected savings from reduced rework; Jakarta LRT saved $14.8M catching 1,200 clashes before construction. System balance: longer asset life, less material waste, resilience for water/utility networks. Moat: 40-year engineer installed base plus asset data in AssetWise; Autodesk is the main rival but Bentley leads heavy civil. Low dilution, modest leverage.

## IOT (Samsara) — price $39.84, mcap $23.22B (Aug 26, 2026)
Connected Operations platform (telematics, video safety, equipment monitoring) for physical fleets. Q1 FY27 (Jun 2026): revenue $479M +31%; ARR ~$2.0B +30%; third straight GAAP-profitable quarter; non-GAAP op margin 19%; 3,363 customers >$100K ARR (+37% YoY cohort growth). Real cost compression is the core product: customers show 47% crash reduction (63% with video safety) against $16.5K-$74K per crash; 40% less idling (~$2,500/vehicle/yr fuel); ITF Group saved $4M fuel + $4.4M insurance; AO cut fleet costs 16% and insurance premiums 25%. System balance: fewer crashes, lower fuel burn/emissions across ~600K+ connected assets. Moat: largest connected-operations dataset feeding AI models; rivals Geotab/Motive/Verizon Connect keep pricing honest. FY27 guide $2.005-2.013B (+23-24%).

## TTAN (ServiceTitan) — price $92.23, mcap $8.80B (Aug 26, 2026)
End-to-end operating platform for trades contractors (HVAC, plumbing, electrical; plus FieldRoutes, Aspire). Q1 FY27 (Jun 2026): revenue $268.8M +25% (subscription $202M +24%, usage/payments $58.5M +29%); non-GAAP op margin 15.2%; GAAP still loss-making (TTM net -$136M, EPS -$1.46); FY27 guide $1.13-1.14B; raised on AI "Max" adoption. Cost-compression verdict is mixed: scheduling/dispatch and AI call automation trim wasted truck rolls and office labor, but the marketed ROI is mostly contractor revenue uplift (bigger tickets, higher close rates, payments take-rate) — extraction from homeowners rather than clear system-cost reduction; little quantified empty-mile/fuel evidence. Moat: deep vertical workflows and high switching costs; rivals Jobber/Housecall Pro at SMB end.
