Q2 2026 revenue $570.3M, +28.9% GAAP but only +4.7% pro forma adjusted constant currency (growth mostly from the Biotronik Vascular Intervention acquisition). Adjusted EPS $1.76 (+1.7%). Permanent hospital need and real regulatory/installed-base moat (Arrow, LMA), but underlying growth is ~4%, portfolio churn is heavy, and UroLift's collapse shows weak franchises. Stagnant commercialization vs. compounder bar.

## DMTRF — 3-D Matrix (~$3.96, ~$507M mcap)
Sells PuraStat, a synthetic RADA16 self-assembling peptide hemostatic gel used in GI endoscopy, cardiovascular and general surgery; sold in Europe, Japan, US, Australia. FY2026 (ended April): revenue JPY 10.9B (~$72M), fifth straight year of growth (JPY 1.5B in FY2022, ~7x in four years), and a swing to net profit (EPS JPY 34.88) after years of losses. FDA cleared for GI bleeding (2021) plus a new US marketing approval for radiation-proctitis wound healing; pipeline targets oral mucositis and IBD. Moat: unique peptide-platform IP and multi-region regulatory approvals; main substitute is Cook's Hemospray powder and clips/coagulation, so competition exists but PuraStat's transparency/prophylactic use differentiates. Risks: small cap, long history of equity funding/dilution, single-platform concentration.

## ATRC — AtriCure (~$47.16, ~$2.4B mcap)
Sells surgical atrial-fibrillation ablation (concomitant + hybrid EPi-Sense), AtriClip left-atrial-appendage exclusion, and cryoSPHERE cryo nerve block for post-surgical pain (opioid-sparing). Q2 2026 revenue $153.6M (+12.8%; US +13.6%), return to GAAP profitability ($9M net income, $27M adj EBITDA); FY26 guidance raised to $602-610M revenue, $85-89M adj EBITDA. Real system value: LAA exclusion prevents strokes, cryo nerve block cuts opioid use. Moat: dominant installed base, surgeon training network, clinical evidence (LeAAPS prophylactic-LAA trial could expand market). Threats mounting: Medtronic Penditure CE-marked and Edwards LAA clip FDA-cleared attack AtriClip; catheter PFA (Boston Scientific Farapulse) pressures hybrid ablation; AtriCure is answering with a dual-energy PFA+RF platform (first-in-human Q4 2025, not yet approved).

## DCTH — Delcath Systems (~$17.05, ~$592M mcap)
Sells HEPZATO KIT (US) / CHEMOSAT (EU): percutaneous hepatic perfusion delivering high-dose melphalan directly to the liver, approved for metastatic uveal melanoma. Q2 2026 revenue $29.1M (+20%; HEPZATO volume +30%), 90% gross margin, net income $2.7M; FY26 guidance raised to $104-108M; 31 active centers, targeting 37 by year-end. Only approved PHP system — a real regulatory/procedural moat in its niche. But the approved indication is ultra-rare; growth beyond it depends on unproven trials (Phase 2 DELUMA in liver-dominant metastatic breast cancer dosed first patient July 2026; ASCO combo studies with tebentafusp/nivolumab). ~$170-185k per kit is premium oncology monetization, not cost compression; 340B pricing already trimmed realized price. Long pre-2023 history of failures, reverse splits, and severe dilution.

## CERS — Cerus (~$2.75, ~$550M mcap)
INTERCEPT pathogen-reduction systems for platelets, plasma, and cryoprecipitate (IFC fibrinogen complex) — broad-spectrum inactivation of viruses/bacteria/parasites in donated blood; effectively US standard of care for platelets with razor-blade consumable economics and deep blood-center integration (main rival Terumo Mirasol has limited reach). Q2 2026: total revenue $63.3M, product revenue $57.4M (+10%), gross margin 51.4%, net loss just $2.9M, ninth straight quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA; FY26 product guidance raised to $229-231M (+11-12%) with IFC guided +40-50% ($23-25M). Big catalyst: INTERCEPT Red Blood Cells CE Mark in late-stage TUV-SUD review (clinical module complete) — RBCs are the largest transfusion segment. System value is real: pandemic-proofs blood supply, reduces septic reactions and waste, extends IFC shelf life. Watch: thin ~51% margins, decades of dilution, ~11% core growth.

## NYXH — Nyxoah (~$1.64, ~$164M mcap)
Sells Genio, a battery-free bilateral hypoglossal-nerve-stimulation implant for obstructive sleep apnea (FDA approved Aug 2025; CE-marked). US launch is ramping fast off a tiny base: Q2 2026 US revenue EUR 5.2M (+22% sequential), H1 global EUR 14.0M vs EUR 2.4M a year ago; 180 active US accounts, 262 trained surgeons, prior-auth pipeline +77%; FY26 guidance EUR 36-40M; CMS proposed +12-15% 2027 procedure payments. But economics are brutal: cash EUR 97.8M only after a June 2026 ~$110M raise (heavy dilution at ~$1.60s), Stifel estimates >$200M more needed to reach positive FCF; CEO search underway; escalating patent litigation with dominant incumbent Inspire Medical; GLP-1 obesity drugs (tirzepatide approved for OSA) shrink the addressable pool. Differentiated tech, unproven business.

## SGHT — Sight Sciences (~$5.26, ~$260-286M mcap)
Sells OMNI/SION implant-free interventional-glaucoma devices and TearCare (interventional dry eye, meibomian-gland clearance). Q2 2026 revenue $23.4M (+20%): glaucoma $20.7M (+8%), TearCare $2.7M (+704% on new reimbursement, +98% sequential). FY26 guidance raised to $88-92M (+14-19%); TearCare guide up to $9-11M. Positives: Aetna added 25M covered lives for OMNI/SION (July 2026); OMNI Ultra FDA 510(k) cleared; won final judgment in Alcon litigation; net loss cut 63% to $4.4M; ex-one-time cash burn only $1.4M/quarter with $79.8M cash ($40M debt). Cost-compression story is real (implant-free procedures; treating dry-eye root cause vs lifelong drops). But core IG grows single-digit in a fiercely competitive MIGS market (Glaukos iStent/iDose, AbbVie, Alcon) with demonstrated payer-policy fragility (2023 LCD scare), and the growth thesis leans on early-stage TearCare coverage.

## BB — BlackBerry (~$7.77, ~$4.5B mcap)
Verdict on the QNX question: real growth and moat, not legacy decline. Q1 FY2027 (May 2026 quarter): QNX revenue $72.3M, +26% YoY with adjusted EBITDA $19.3M (+52%); development-license revenue at an eight-quarter high (leading indicator of future royalties); ~$1B royalty backlog of contracted embedded deployments; FY27 QNX guidance raised to $295-312M within total-company $594-621M; first cash-positive fiscal Q1 in nine years. Moat: decades of safety certifications (ISO 26262/IEC 62304-class), massive vehicle installed base, deepening NVIDIA partnership for safety-critical edge AI, and expansion beyond automotive into robotics, medical devices, and industrial automation — pre-certified RTOS compresses OEMs' cost and time to certify software-defined systems. Risks: OEM in-housing and open-source (Android/Linux took infotainment), Secure Comms segment slower, historical strategy churn (Cylance divested 2025).
