## AHICF — Asahi Intecc (85, ADVANCE)
World leader in specialty PTCA/CTO guidewires, micro-catheters and micro-wire components; the reference standard for crossing chronic total occlusions. FY6/2026 (reported Aug 2026): net sales ¥145.4B +21.2%, operating profit ¥45.2B +50.3%, net profit +151.8%; dividend doubled to ¥48.24 — record year, minimal dilution history, historically net-cash. Moat is manufacturing craft: proprietary wire-drawing/torque technology so hard to replicate that Abbott sells Asahi-made wires under license; top CTO-guidewire share globally alongside Abbott/Terumo/Boston in a 65%-concentrated market. Cost compression: cheap wires enable PCI instead of open bypass and let operators cross CTOs without surgery. Export product model (plants in Thailand/Vietnam). Risks: China VBP pricing, thin ADR liquidity. Price ~$21.41, mcap ~$6.7B (stockanalysis.com, ADR quote lags). ## GKOS — Glaukos (82, ADVANCE)
Interventional-glaucoma pioneer: iStent MIGS implants, iDose TR (intracameral travoprost implant, up to 3 years of therapy; FDA approved re-administration Jan 2026), and Epioxa — first epi-on topical keratoconus cross-linking (FDA Oct 2025, launched Mar 2026, permanent J-code Jul 2026). Q2 2026: record revenue $185.6M +50% YoY; iDose TR ~$74M/qtr; non-GAAP gross margin ~85%; FY26 guidance raised to $680–700M. Cost compression: glaucoma-drop non-adherence drives progression to blindness; a 3-year implant removes the compliance failure mode and MIGS/Epioxa avoid trabeculectomy and corneal transplant — though iDose is premium-priced vs. generic drops, so net compression is real but partial. Moat: NDA drug-device combos, 12 commercial products, 13 pipeline programs, no direct sustained-travoprost competitor (Durysta lasts months). Price ~$189.45, mcap ~$10.9B. ## BOEUF — Bonesupport (84, ADVANCE)
Swedish maker of CERAMENT, the injectable antibiotic-eluting ceramic bone-graft substitute (G=gentamicin, V=vancomycin) that remodels to real bone while killing osteomyelitis. Q2 2026: net sales SEK 355.7M +25% (+30% CER), gross margin 90.8%, adjusted operating margin ~29% — profitable and self-funding. Cost compression is exceptional: single-stage treatment saves ~$27,943/patient vs antibiotic PMMA beads; registry data show amputation rate 2% vs 18% standard care and hospital stay 12.6 vs 25.1 days; recurrence ~4% vs ~20%. Moat: only FDA-authorized (De Novo) antibiotic-eluting injectable bone substitute, backed by SOLARIO/FORTIFY-class evidence; drug-device regulatory barrier. CERAMENT V US approval in progress; US segment +25%. Risk: single-platform concentration. Price ~$23.49 (BOEUF OTC; BONEX.ST SEK 221.40), mcap ~$1.56B. ## BFLY — Butterfly Network (72, CUT)
Ultrasound-on-Chip handheld POCUS (iQ3, $3,899) plus software subscriptions, Octiv chip licensing, and Butterfly Garden SDK. Q2 2026: record revenue $32.6M +39%; adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed 78% to -$1.4M; FY26 guidance raised to $119–123M (+22–26%); cash $125M with ~$13M quarterly use; TTM net loss ~$75M. Cost compression is genuine — a whole-body $4k probe replaces $30k–100k cart systems and pushes imaging into primary care, home care and low-resource settings (Brazil expansion announced). But the moat is contested: Exo Iris (silicon pMUT), GE Vscan Air, Clarius, Philips Lumify, EchoNous, Mindray all compete; blind image-quality surveys show narrowing gaps and substitution is easy. SPAC-era dilution history; never profitable. Stock up ~500% YoY to $8.64, mcap $2.30B (Aug 26, 2026). ## PODD — Insulet (71, CUT)
Omnipod 5: the only FDA-cleared tubeless patch pump with automated insulin delivery for type 1 and type 2 diabetes, sold as 3-day disposable pods via the pharmacy channel. Q2 2026: revenue $801.7M +23.5% (US +20%, intl +32.9% CER), profitable with expanding margins — but FY26 guidance was cut (US Omnipod to 17–19%) on type 2 patient retention problems, and 2027 outlook of mid-teens growth disappointed; stock fell ~12% and is down ~56% in a year ($146.87, mcap ~$10.2B, Aug 21 2026). Cost compression is partial: AID improves time-in-range and cuts hypoglycemia/DKA admissions with no big upfront pump cost, but recurring consumables are pricier than MDI and GLP-1s attack the T2 insulin pool. Moat eroding: Tandem (Mobi tubeless, Sigi), Medtronic and Beta Bionics all have patch pumps coming; switching costs are low; single-product concentration. ## DXCM — DexCom (81, ADVANCE)
CGM pure-play (G7, 15-day G7, OTC Stelo, Flex; multi-analyte G8 targeted late 2027/early 2028). Q2 2026: revenue $1.31B +13.1% (intl +19%); FY26 guidance raised to $5.18–5.25B with adjusted operating margin 23.5–24% and EBITDA margin ~32% — profitable at scale. Price $92.33, mcap ~$34.85B (Aug 24, 2026). Cost compression: CGM demonstrably cuts severe hypoglycemia, ER visits and A1c; 15-day sensor lowers annualized cost; $50-class OTC Stelo extends prevention to prediabetes/T2 non-insulin users. Moat: effective duopoly with Abbott Libre behind heavy sensor-manufacturing and regulatory barriers, deepened by the June 2026 Abbott–Dexcom 10-year royalty-free patent cross-license; integrated with every major AID pump. Risks: Abbott's scale/price lead and dual glucose-ketone Libre Duo; growth decelerated to low teens. CGM market seen growing ~15%/yr to $41B by 2033. ## PEN — Penumbra (79, CUT)
World-leading thrombectomy franchise (Indigo/Lightning computer-assisted vacuum aspiration for stroke, PE, DVT; THUNDERBOLT stroke clearance in 2026) plus embolization/access. Q2 2026: revenue $390.0M +14.9% (thrombectomy $259M +12.5%; embolization $131M +20%); TTM revenue $1.50B, profitable (EPS $4.07). Fundamentals would score well: mechanical clot removal averts stroke disability and replaces ICU-intensive lytic therapy with bleeding risk — genuine cost-per-outcome compression — behind a strong installed base and clinical-data moat (rivals: Stryker/Inari, Medtronic). Decisive fact: Boston Scientific agreed Jan 15, 2026 to acquire Penumbra for $14.5B at $374/share, closing expected H2 2026; no FY guidance given. Stock $323.05, mcap $12.72B (Aug 26, 2026) — a merger-arb position, not a compounder; upside capped at deal price. ## IRTC — iRhythm (79, ADVANCE)
Zio long-term continuous ECG patch plus AI analysis service for arrhythmia (AFib) diagnosis. Q2 2026: revenue $224.2M +20.1% — seventh straight quarter above 20%; FY26 guidance raised to $880–890M (+18–19%) with adjusted EBITDA margin 13–14%; GAAP still slightly negative (TTM net -$14M, improving fast). Price $119.15, mcap $3.93B (Aug 26, 2026). Cost compression is core: Medicare comparative-effectiveness data show LTCM/Zio delivers the lowest 1-year incremental expenditure of all monitoring strategies, 20% higher arrhythmia detection and 3.5x fewer retests vs rivals; FDA-cleared third-gen algorithm halves technician review time (~$100M savings over 5 yrs). Expansion: VitalConnect acquisition ($288M) opens full MCT and hospital-to-home; primary-care and international channels growing; US market <30% penetrated. Risks: Philips BioTelemetry/Boston Scientific Preventice competition; CMS reimbursement-rate concentration.
