# Round 4 notes — Group 05 (food systems)

## MHGVY — Mowi ASA ($22.66, ~$11.8B mcap, Aug 26 2026)
World's largest Atlantic salmon farmer, fully integrated (feed, farming, processing, branded). Q2 2026: record EUR 1.6B revenue, operational EBIT EUR 231M (+23%), record 150k GWT harvest (+13%), farming cost down 3.5% to EUR 5.20/kg. TTM revenue $6.99B (+7.5%), net income $728M, PE ~16, 2.7% yield, leverage ~2x EBITDA, no dilution. Honest aquaculture judgment: salmon FCR ~1.15 makes it the most feed-efficient animal protein — genuine system efficiency vs terrestrial meat — but open-net pens concentrate waste, sea lice and chemical use draw criticism (DeSmog). Mitigation is real: EUR 400M capex, four new closed-containment post-smolt systems, 50M post-smolt target. Scarce licenses = moat. ADVANCE.

## DAR — Darling Ingredients ($66.94, ~$10.1B mcap, Aug 2026)
World's largest publicly traded renderer: collects animal by-products, used cooking oil and bakery waste, converts to feed/food/fuel ingredients; 50/50 Diamond Green Diesel JV with Valero (renewable diesel + SAF). Q2 2026: net sales $1.7B (vs $1.5B), net income $387M (vs $13M), combined adj. EBITDA $742M (vs $250M) as finalized RVO revived renewable diesel margins; DGD produced 356M gallons; net leverage cut to 2.3x. Moat: dense collection-route logistics and long-term slaughterhouse contracts, near-impossible to replicate. Genuine circularity — upcycles billions of lbs of waste — though volumes depend on industrial meat throughput and fuel margins on policy (RVO/45Z). Not a factory-farm waste-lagoon model; it is the disposal solution. ADVANCE.

## ENGCF — engcon AB ($8.50, ~$1.06B mcap, Aug 2026)

Competitors Rototilt, Steelwrist, Kinshofer; engcon holds leading share and installed-base/control-system stickiness. PE ~41 rich; stock -34% y/y. No meaningful dilution; profitable through cycle. ## OCDGF — Ocado Group (~$3.23 / 239p, ~£2.1B (~$2.9B) mcap, Aug 2026)
Licenses robotic grocery customer-fulfilment-centre (CFC) tech to retailers; owns 50% of Ocado Retail (UK) and Ocado Logistics. H1 2026 (July 15): Tech Solutions revenue +5% LFL but recurring fees -3% including Kroger and Sobeys CFC closures; underlying cash flow negative £147M; shares sank 19%; no store-based automation deals signed and prospects delaying to 2028+. CFC tech genuinely efficient (268 units/hour, +12%), but 25 years without sustained group profit, chronic dilution, partner churn, and capex-heavy deployments mean commercialization is stalling, not compounding. Hard-rejection fit: stagnant commercialization. CUT.

## PTKFY — PT Kalbe Farma ($9.49 ADR, ~$2.06B mcap, Aug 2026)
Indonesia's largest pharma/consumer-health/nutritionals group with the Enseval distribution arm reaching 1M+ outlets across the archipelago — the real moat. Q1 2026: net sales Rp9.68T (+10.1%) but net income -4.4% on raw-material costs, rupiah depreciation and mix; H1 2026 revenue +14%. TTM revenue $2.11B (+13%), net income ~$202M, PE ~10, net cash, no dilution, 1.4% yield; stock -46% y/y (mcap fell from ~$3.6B). ## BMBRF — BIM Birlesik Magazalar ($5.75 OTC, ~$9.4B mcap, Aug 2026)
Turkey's dominant hard-discount grocer (limited-SKU, heavy private label, lowest food prices), ~13,000+ stores plus FILE format and international replication in Morocco and Egypt. Q2 2026: sales TRY 221.9B (~+10% y/y in real terms), net income TRY 8.02B vs TRY 3.56B — a broad beat across profit/revenue/operating earnings that drew institutional buying; H1 sales TRY 449.7B. Model IS cost compression: scale purchasing, logistics density and private label deliver cheapest calories to lower-income households in an inflation-battered economy. Moat vs A101/Sok is scale and price leadership rather than structural; expansion is capex store rollout but proven exportable. Self-funded, decades profitable, no dilution; key risks are Turkish macro/FX and hyperinflation accounting. ADVANCE.

## ICCC — ImmuCell Corp ($11.23, ~$90M mcap, Aug 2026)
Maine animal-health micro-cap; First Defense is the only USDA-licensed colostrum-derived passive-antibody product preventing calf scours (E. coli/corona/rotavirus) — genuine prevention that cuts antibiotic use and calf mortality. Q2 2026: total sales $7.2M (+11.5%), US First Defense sales $6.2M (+27.7%) after a 50% sales-force expansion; market share 19% vs 15% in Dec 2025; net income $1.8M; board approved ~$3.5M drying-capacity expansion (12 months). But Re-Tain (nisin mastitis alternative) was shelved after an FDA Incomplete Letter tied to contract-manufacturer deficiencies — the breakthrough optionality is gone. What remains is a well-moated but tiny, capacity-bound, US-centric niche (partial substitution by dam vaccination) with history of dilution. Too small/slow for a Top-25 compounder list. CUT.
