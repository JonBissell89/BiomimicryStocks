# Final Round protocol

## Deep track (sub-$10 finalists — these can enter the Top 25)
For EACH company, verify with primary sources (latest 10-Q/6-K/quarterly report, IR pages):
1. EXACT current share price of the named US-traded line, ADR/F-share ratio if applicable,
   market cap of the whole company, average daily dollar liquidity of the US line
2. Latest reported quarter: revenue, growth %, gross margin, operating income/EBITDA, FCF
3. Dilution: share count today vs ~3 years ago (% change); any active ATM/shelf/converts
4. Cash, debt, runway; financing dependence
5. Top 2 competitors and realistic displacement risk; unique IP/regulatory moat status
6. Global expansion status and mechanism (countries, partners, OEMs, distributors)
7. Concrete upcoming catalysts (next 12 months) with dates where known
8. Any corporate actions (pending buyout/delisting = disqualify and say so)
9. Final 8-dimension scores (permanent_need 15, system_balance 15, cost_compression 15,
   moat 15, proven_functions 10, scale_velocity 10, expansion_signal 10, survivability 10)
10. main_risk (<=15 words) and top25_case (<=40 words)

## Light track (over-$10 finalists — Watch Below $10 ranking)
Verify price/cap, latest quarter one-liner, one catalyst, confirm or adjust the R4 total
score with one sentence of justification, one-line why-watch.

Both tracks: honest numbers with sources; if a claim from earlier rounds is wrong, correct it.
