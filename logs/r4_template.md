# Round 4 research protocol (identical for every company)

For EACH assigned company, research with web search (2-4 focused searches each; prefer primary
sources: latest 10-K/10-Q/annual report, company IR/newsroom, regulator databases):
1. What it sells today; latest revenue growth, gross margin direction, cash/debt, dilution history
2. Primary competitors and how easily customers could substitute the product
3. Unique IP / regulatory moat / manufacturing difficulty / installed base / distribution edge
4. Whether it lowers total real cost per useful outcome in its system (cost compression)
5. Global deployment model: product/platform/licensing vs. localized capex-heavy expansion
6. Current expansion signal: recent orders, backlog, approvals, partnerships, capacity, hiring
7. Disruption risk: could a technology shift erase the opportunity?
8. Does its growth make the overall system healthier (balance, circularity, resilience)?
9. CURRENT share price and market cap (verify — do not trust stale data)

Score the 8 dimensions with these maxima (the universal 100-pt framework):
  permanent_need 15 | system_balance 15 | cost_compression 15 | moat 15 |
  proven_functions 10 | scale_velocity 10 | expansion_signal 10 | survivability 10

Hard-rejection rules override scores: economics depending on war/weapons, addiction,
artificial scarcity, planned obsolescence, chronic-sickness monetization, broken-system
maintenance, water-scarcity profiteering, single unproven breakthrough, excessive dilution,
stagnant commercialization, undifferentiated commodity.

Verdict: ADVANCE (genuinely strong candidate for a Top-25 of system-improving compounders)
or CUT (state the single decisive reason). Be strict — roughly half should be CUT.
IGNORE share price in scoring/verdict (note it only in the data fields; the $10 gate is applied later).
